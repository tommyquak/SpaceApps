CONTENTS:

1. InSAR_DisplacementTimeseries_<LandUse>-<SiteName>.csv
Displacement time series derived from ALOS-2 InSAR at 8 validation sites, projected from the line-of-sight to vertical direction. [Units = cm]

2. InSAR_LongTermRate_20181012-20240809.tif
Long-term rates derived from ALOS-2 InSAR covering peat areas of Central Kalimantan, Indonesia, projected from the line-of-sight to vertical direction. Long-term rates are computed from the linear best-fit of the displacement time series. Poor quality data over decorrelated areas (mean phase standard deviation > 0.7cm) are excluded. [Units = m]

3. InSAR_MaxSubsidence-2019ElNino.tif 
Maximum subsidence derived from ALOS-2 InSAR during the 2019 El Niño, covering peat areas of Central Kalimantan, Indonesia, projected from the line-of-sight to vertical direction. Maximum subsidence is derived from the lowest displacement detected during the 2019 El Niño (15 May 2019 to 16 November 2019) with respect to the average of displacements prior to the 2019 El Niño (12 October 2018 to 15 May 2019). Poor quality data over decorrelated areas (mean phase standard deviation > 0.7cm) are excluded. [Units = m]
